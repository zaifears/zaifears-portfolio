## Anti-patterns to eliminate

1. **Generic blue gradient backgrounds** — the animated blob gradients are fine as subtle accents; full-bleed gradient washes are not.
2. **Hollow badge/chip soup** — every skill or tag does not need a rounded pill badge.
3. **Vague CTAs** — "Learn More", "Explore", "Get Started" are prohibited. Every CTA must state what happens: "View StockSimulatorBD", "See competition wins", "Read the case".
4. **Excessive card nesting** — no cards inside cards.
5. **Cramped sections** — generous white space signals confidence, not emptiness.

## Competition/bizcomp microsites

Treated as **standalone islands** with their own chrome. Impeccable commands run on one route at a time — don't assume styles from `/bizcomp/accfinity` generalize to the main portfolio.

## Internal tools

`/meetup`, `/zakat-calculation`, `/zakat-report`, `/bride-selector` are full-screen, navbar-free, private tools. Impeccable should apply minimal interference here — correctness over aesthetics.
